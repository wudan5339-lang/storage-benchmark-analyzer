6. Test Notes

Data interpretation: The JSON file contains overall benchmark statistics, while the LOG files contain time-series measurements. Their averages may differ because they use different aggregation methods.

Statistical interpretation: Descriptive statistics and percentiles in Section 3 are calculated from time-series log samples. They describe the distribution of recorded interval measurements, rather than individual I/O operations. The percentile values in Section 2 are fio's overall latency statistics.

Correlation limitation: IOPS and bandwidth are related through block size. Their correlation does not establish a causal relationship.

Test limitation: This project uses direct=0 in its current sample configuration. Therefore, operating system page cache may influence the results. The measurements are intended to demonstrate the data parsing and analysis workflow, rather than provide a definitive physical SSD performance benchmark.
